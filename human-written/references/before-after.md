# Before and after

One example of the target change. Use it to calibrate, not to copy. Note that the rewrite adds no facts: every number and name comes from the original.

## Before (AI-sounding)

In today's fast-paced digital landscape, remote work has emerged as a pivotal force reshaping how organizations operate. It's not just a temporary trend; it's a fundamental shift in the tapestry of modern employment. According to a 2023 Gallup survey, 52% of remote-capable employees now work in a hybrid arrangement, highlighting the growing importance of flexibility. Moreover, experts say that companies leveraging flexible work arrangements can unlock significant productivity gains, fostering a more engaged, motivated, and resilient workforce. The firm Acme Corp, for example, cut attrition by 18% after the organization adopted a three-day office week. Despite challenges such as communication barriers, remote work continues to thrive. In conclusion, it is important to note that the future of work is here to stay.

## After (human-sounding)

Hybrid work is now the default for office jobs. A 2023 Gallup survey found that 52% of employees who can work remotely split their week between home and office.

Some companies have numbers to show for it. Acme Corp cut attrition by 18% after it moved to a three-day office week. The main drawback is communication, which gets harder when people are not in the same room.

## What changed
- Cut the opening cliche and the words "landscape", "pivotal", "tapestry", "leveraging", "unlock", and "fostering".
- Replaced "It's not just X; it's Y" with a plain statement.
- Removed the trailing "highlighting the growing importance" clause.
- Dropped "experts say" because the source names no expert. The Gallup and Acme facts stay because the source has them.
- Stopped cycling "the firm", "Acme Corp", and "the organization" for one company.
- Removed the adjective triplet, the "Despite challenges" line, and the summary closer.
- Expanded the contraction and removed "Moreover".
- The rewrite is shorter. That is normal.
