# Banned and flagged phrases

Edit this file freely. The skill reads it before every rewrite. Remove or rewrite every hit. Do not swap in a synonym from the same family; fix the sentence.

A word on this list is not wrong in every context. "Landscape" is fine in an article about painting. Flag a word when it is used as filler, as a metaphor, or for emphasis.

## Words from the original research
underscore, delve, tapestry, pivotal, harness, illuminate, testament, landscape, "In conclusion"

## Overused AI vocabulary
Verbs: delve, underscore, showcase, highlight (as commentary), foster, leverage, harness, unlock, unleash, empower, elevate, enhance, bolster, streamline, navigate (figurative), embark, unveil, unravel, illuminate, elucidate, encapsulate, encompass, transcend, revolutionize, resonate, spearhead, garner, pave the way, shed light on

Adjectives: crucial, vital, pivotal, essential (for emphasis), key (for emphasis), comprehensive, robust, seamless, intricate, multifaceted, nuanced (as filler), dynamic, vibrant, rich, profound, transformative, groundbreaking, cutting-edge, innovative, unparalleled, remarkable, noteworthy, commendable, meticulous, invaluable, compelling, ever-evolving, burgeoning

Nouns: tapestry, landscape (figurative), realm, journey (figurative), testament, interplay, intricacies, nuances, insights (as filler), synergy, paradigm, beacon, cornerstone, game-changer, symphony, mosaic

Adverbs: notably, particularly (as filler), seamlessly, meticulously, ultimately, quietly (for drama), truly, genuinely, arguably

## Reflexive transitions
moreover, furthermore, additionally, in addition, consequently, conversely, that said, with that in mind, on the other hand (as a reflex)

## Hedging, filler, and signposting
- "It is important to note that"
- "It is worth noting / mentioning"
- "It should be noted"
- "In today's fast-paced world" / "In today's digital age"
- "In the realm of"
- "When it comes to"
- "At the end of the day"
- "Needless to say"
- "In this article, we will"
- "Let us dive in" / "Let us explore" / "Let us take a closer look"
- "Here is why:" / "Here is the thing:"
- "No discussion of X would be complete without"

## Significance inflation
- "plays a crucial/vital/pivotal/key role"
- "stands as a testament to" / "serves as a testament to"
- "serves as a reminder"
- "marks a significant shift" / "a watershed moment" / "a key turning point"
- "deeply rooted" / "lasting legacy" / "solidifies its place"
- "leaves an indelible mark"

## Promotional language
breathtaking, stunning, must-visit, nestled, hidden gem, rich cultural heritage, rich cultural tapestry, vibrant community, world-class, state-of-the-art

## Sentence formulas
- "It is not just X, it is Y" / "Not X, but Y" / "Not only X but also Y"
- "This is not about X. It is about Y."
- "..., highlighting / underscoring / reflecting / ensuring / emphasizing ..." as a trailing clause
- "ranging from X to Y" (when not a real scale)
- "Despite these challenges, X continues to thrive"
- "The future of X looks bright"
- "Whether you are A, B, or C, ..."
- "The result? ..." / "The catch? ..." / "The kicker? ..." / "The brutal truth? ..."
- "And honestly? ..."

## Vague attribution
experts say, studies show, research suggests, many believe, some argue, critics argue, observers note, industry reports suggest (all without a named source)

## Chatbot and letter residue
"Great question", "Certainly!", "Absolutely!", "Sure! Here is", "I would be happy to", "I hope this helps", "Let me know if you would like", "I hope this message finds you well", "As an AI", "As of my last update", "[insert ...]"

## Openers and closers
- Dramatic scene-setting openers (Wild West, journey, or storm metaphors)
- "In conclusion", "In summary", "To sum up", "Overall", "In short" as a paragraph opener
- A last paragraph that only restates the introduction
- A motivational call to action the body did not earn
